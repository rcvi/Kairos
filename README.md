# Kairos

Welcome to Kairos, an extremely basic browser extension for when you want your browser to mind its own business. Check out the official landing page and test drive it at https://rcvi.github.io/Kairos/test.

---

## What Does Kairos Stop Tracking?
Are you tired of websites gaslighting you for daring to look at another tab for half a second? Kairos blocks detection for:
* **Tab switching & minimizing** (Because what you do on your own screen is your business).
* **Window focus loss** (Clicking off the tab? Never heard of it).
* **Window resizing** (Split-screen away without the page throwing a tantrum).
* **Fullscreen exiting** (Convincing the web page you are trapped in fullscreen forever).
* **User inactivity idle counters** (Sleep is for the weak; pretending to be active is an art form).
* **Copy and paste detection** (Because typing things out character-by-character went out of style in 2004).

## How to Install 

If you're ready to take the plunge, follow these steps to install the extension:

* **Download & Extract:** Grab the extension ZIP file from the Kairos Releases https://github.com/rcvi/kairos/releases or clone the GitHub Repository https://github.com/rcvi/kairos, then extract it into a dedicated folder.
* **Open Extensions:** Open your Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, etc.) and type `chrome://extensions/` into your address bar.
* **Toggle Developer Mode:** Flip the switch for Developer mode in the top-right corner.
* **Load It:** Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left corner.
* **Select Folder:** Choose the folder containing your `manifest.json` file.
* **Profit:** Now don't dualrun spotify alongside your exam window, that would be BAD!
